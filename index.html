About Me

I'm a bioinformatician at the Burnet Institute working with Associate Professor Mark Ziemann on computational proteomics. My research focuses on benchmarking software workflows for Data Independent Acquisition (DIA) proteomics data generated on the Orbitrap Astral mass spectrometer.

My work bridges wet lab knowledge and computational analysis — evaluating how different software tools perform on the same dataset, comparing protein detection rates, handling of missing values, and reproducibility across sample groups.

I completed my studies at Deakin University, where I developed skills in bioinformatics, statistical analysis, and high-performance computing for life science applications.

Burnet Institute · Deakin University · 2024–2025
